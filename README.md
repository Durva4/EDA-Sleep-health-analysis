# Sleep Health and Lifestyle - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

This project conducts an in-depth Exploratory Data Analysis on a dataset of 374 individuals to understand the factors affecting sleep quality and general well-being. By examining variables such as occupation, BMI, and physical activity, this analysis identifies key trends that contribute to better sleep health.

## 📊 Project Overview
The primary goal is to analyze how lifestyle choices and demographic factors influence sleep duration and quality. This project answers critical questions regarding stress levels across professions and the impact of physical health (BMI) on sleep patterns.

## 🗂️ Dataset Highlights
The dataset contains 15 columns, including:
* **Demographics:** Gender, Age, and Occupation.
* **Sleep Metrics:** Sleep Duration (hours) and Quality of Sleep (1-10 scale).
* **Health Indicators:** BMI Category, Blood Pressure, and Heart Rate.
* **Lifestyle Factors:** Physical Activity Level, Stress Level, and Daily Steps.

## 🚀 Key Insights
* **Occupational Stress:** Sales Representatives and Scientists report the highest average stress levels, while Engineers and Accountants report the lowest.
* **BMI & Activity:** Individuals in the "Obese" category show significantly lower daily step counts compared to "Normal" or "Overweight" groups.
* **Age Trends:** Nurses in the dataset are generally older (avg. age ~52), while Doctors represent a younger demographic (avg. age ~33).
* **Sleep Quality:** A strong correlation exists between higher physical activity and improved quality of sleep.
